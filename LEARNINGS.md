## 1. Where we start (our own numbers)

Source: Metricool analytics for brand getdirectops, pulled 2026-11-13.

- Instagram followers: **1**. Account reach over the last 28 days: 0.
- The account posted once before: 12 feed posts on Instagram and 13 on Facebook,
  one a day, 6 to 18 June 2026.
- Best Instagram post of that run: reach 2, views 8, 1 like. Most posts: reach 0 or 1.
  Zero saves, zero comments, zero follows across the run. Facebook: reach 0 or 1 per post.
- What that run looked like: 9 to 11 hashtags per caption, emoji in most lines, "AI concierge"
  for Airbnb hosts, a booking-calendar link on every Facebook post, a founding-customer offer on day 6.

What to take from it:

1. **Volume was not the constraint. Distribution was.** Twelve posts in thirteen days reached
   nobody, because nobody was there to reach and nothing gave Instagram a reason to show them further.
2. **Per-post numbers are noise for now.** Until a post regularly reaches about 100 people,
   a difference between two posts tells us nothing. For the first weeks, watch account-level
   reach, follows and profile visits, not which caption "won".
3. **The feed needs people before it needs optimising.** See the note to Sebastian in `CONTROL.md`.

### What the accounts show

- **Vendor accounts earn very little here, whatever their size.** Five software companies with
  3K to 38K followers all sit between about 650 and 900 plays per Reel, with 5 to 30 likes.
  Follower count buys almost nothing. A small account that says something useful can match them.
- **The one account that travels is a person with news.** @boostlyuk's Reels on Airbnb policy
  changes drew 9K to 22K plays and 100 to 470 comments in the same week that vendor Reels drew
  hundreds. Three things separate it: a founder's face, a topic that changes the operator's week,
  and "comment a word and I'll send it" as the call to action.
- **The vendor posts that do best start from a situation, not a feature.** Mews's better Reels open
  on "a guest pays for early check-in, but the room isn't ready" or "room allocation is Tetris every
  morning". Their podcast clips do worse. Cloudbeds's opinion pieces ("don't hit the discount button")
  sit mid-table.
- **Creators outperform the brands that hire them.** The top Reels on @lodgify and @guestyinc are
  hosts speaking in their own voice. Not something we can copy without real operators, and never with invented ones.
- **Everyone says "AI agent" and "imagine".** Plain operator language is open ground. That is our lane.
- One caution: Hostaway posted, a week ago, the same observation as our post p003 (urgent messages
  buried among routine ones). It is a common truth of the trade, arrived at separately. Ours stays
  because the night-log treatment is our own, but check the list above before each plan refresh so
  we don't echo a larger account by accident.

## 3. Formats and platform mechanics

"Checked" means the source was opened and read on that date. "Read once" means a research
pass summarised it on 2026-11-13; re-open it before repeating the number anywhere public.

**Instagram**

- Carousels earn about nine times the saves of single images; single-image reach fell about 22%
  year on year; captions with a question earned about 37% more comments, and captions that ask for
  a comment about 200% more. Metricool study of 24.4M posts, published 16 Jun 2026.
  https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-study-2026/ (checked 2026-11-13)
- In the same study, posts with at least one hashtag had 31.7% fewer views than average.
  A correlation, not a cause, but there is no evidence hashtags help. (checked 2026-11-13)
- Engagement by format, by followers: carousels 0.55%, Reels 0.52%, images 0.37% (2025).
  Socialinsider, 20 Feb 2026. https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-benchmarks/ (read once)
- Engagement by reach: carousels 6.9%, images 4.4%, Reels 3.3%; Reels reach further than carousels.
  Buffer, 19 Mar 2026. https://buffer.com/resources/data-best-content-format-social-media/ (read once)
- Instagram limits hashtags to five per caption and advises fewer, targeted ones.
  Social Media Today, 18 Dec 2025. https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagrams-implementing-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/ (read once).
  Whether first-comment hashtags count towards the five is not confirmed; we count them anyway.
- Ranking: "watch time, likes and sends" matter most, and sends matter most for reaching people who
  don't follow you. Adam Mosseri, reported 22 Jan 2025. https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-2025/738034/ (read once)
- Sizes: 1080x1350 is still standard. The profile grid crops to 3:4, which trims about 34px from
  each side. Our templates keep an 88px margin. Hootsuite, 2 Nov 2026. https://blog.hootsuite.com/social-media-image-sizes-guide/ (read once)

**Facebook**

- Link posts are the weakest format on Pages (0.05% engagement against 0.15% for images).
  Socialinsider, 23 Mar 2026. https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/facebook-benchmarks/ (read once)
- Meta is testing a cap of two link posts a month for some Pages and professional accounts that
  don't pay for a subscription; the test began in December 2025 and has widened.
  Social Media Today, 17 Sep 2026. https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/facebook-pages-get-charged-for-link-posts/ (checked 2026-11-13).
  The article does not say whether a photo post with a typed link counts, or whether comments are exempt.
- 98.7% of US feed views were of posts without an outbound link. Meta, Widely Viewed Content Report,
  Q1 2026. https://transparency.meta.com/reports/widely-viewed-content-report/ (read once)

**Timing**

- Metricool's own best-time data for our Facebook page (pulled 2026-11-13, Dubai time): the peak is
  10:00 to 12:00 on Monday to Wednesday, with a second plateau from 15:00 to 18:00. Weekends run at about half.
  Instagram returns no data yet: there are no followers to measure.
- Travel and hospitality brands, local time: Monday to Wednesday late morning to mid-afternoon,
  weekends weakest. Sprout Social, 31 Mar 2026. https://sproutsocial.com/insights/data/instagram-best-times-to-post/ (read once).
  That study is of consumer travel brands, not of operators. No study of B2B hospitality timing was found.

## 4. Decisions for our feed (v0)

1. **Lead with carousels and situations.** Carousels for anything a reader would save. Every post
   opens on a time, a room or a guest, never on the product.
2. **Three or four precise hashtags, in the first comment, never more than five.** From week 3,
   alternate posts with and without hashtags and compare reach.
3. **End most captions on a plain question.** It is the one caption habit with data behind it.
4. **Two slots, Dubai time:** 12:00 (09:00 in Central Europe) and 15:00 (lunch in Central Europe,
   afternoon in the UAE). Alternate them and let the weekly review pick.
5. **Facebook link:** in the caption on at most two posts a month, in the first comment on the rest.
   That keeps us inside Meta's cap if this page is in the test, and gives us both placements to compare.
6. **No Reels until `bank/` has real footage.** The accounts above show Reels are where reach is,
   and a person on camera is what carries them. That is a decision for Sebastian, not for a template.
7. **Pillar mix stays at the starting split** (40 / 20 / 15 / 15 / 10) until there is enough reach to judge.
