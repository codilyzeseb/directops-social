# bank

Real material for the feed. Anything dropped here gets used before a designed graphic does:
it is what makes the feed look like people run it.

**This repository is public.** Only put here what could be posted as it is.
No guest names, no faces without consent, no booking details, no prices, no internal dashboards
showing real guest messages.

## What helps most

- **Photos**, phone quality is fine: a reception desk at night, a key box, a welcome note on a bed,
  the view at breakfast, a laptop on the desk. Landscape or portrait, 1500px or more on the short side.
- **Screenshots or screen recordings** of the desk answering on one of our own properties, with any
  guest detail removed.
- **Short clips**, 5 to 20 seconds, vertical. Reels are only made from what is here.
- **`notes.md`**: a few lines whenever something ships or something happens at a property.
  "Behind the build" posts are written only from this file or from what is already public.

## Naming

`YYYY-MM-DD-what-it-is.jpg`, for example `2026-11-20-key-box-gate.jpg`.
Add a line to `notes.md` saying where it was taken and whether the property may be named.

## What happens to it

A file is used once, then noted in `LOG.md` with the post id. Nothing here is edited
beyond cropping and a brand caption strip. Nothing is ever generated to stand in for it.
